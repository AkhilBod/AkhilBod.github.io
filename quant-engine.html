Quantitative Investment Engine

A backtesting and research engine for the portfolio team at Federated Hermes. Won first place at their internal hackathon.

Event
Federated Hermes hackathon
Result
1st place
Stack
Python, SQL, PySpark, Databricks, AWS

The portfolio team had more trading ideas than time to check them. This engine ranks ideas against historical market data, so the team can see which ones are worth a closer look before anyone touches client money.

It runs on Databricks with Python, SQL, and PySpark. Strategies get backtested the same way every time, and the recurring analysis and reporting the team used to do by hand became pipelines they can rerun.

It won first place at the hackathon, and the projected savings from automating that recurring work were about $75K a year.
