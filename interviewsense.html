InterviewSense

Overview

InterviewSense is an AI interview prep platform used by 12K+ people to practice technical and behavioral interviews with real-time feedback. It turns mock interviews into a tighter feedback loop: answer, evaluate, learn what was weak, and improve the next attempt.

Why it matters

Most interview prep tools stop at question banks. InterviewSense focuses on the live performance layer: communication clarity, answer structure, technical correctness, and repeatable improvement across sessions.

Built with

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, OpenAI API, React, Docker, AWS
