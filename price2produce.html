Price2Produce

Overview

Price2Produce is a produce price comparison tool built to make grocery shopping a little easier and more transparent. It pulls pricing across 50+ retailers and surfaces cheaper options quickly enough to be useful while someone is actually planning a grocery run.

Traction

Reached 120+ weekly users through a practical use case: helping students and families compare produce prices without manually checking every grocery site.

Built with

Python, BeautifulSoup, asyncio, Playwright, FastAPI, React
