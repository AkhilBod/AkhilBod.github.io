Price2Produce

A produce price comparison tool that checks 50+ grocery retailers in under a minute.

Users
120+ weekly
Stack
Python, asyncio, Playwright, BeautifulSoup, Flask, React

Price2Produce saves you from checking five grocery sites before every trip. You search for an item and it shows you who has it cheapest nearby.

The scrapers run concurrently with asyncio and Playwright, so pulling prices from 50+ retailers takes under a minute. The annoying part was keeping them alive, because retail sites change their markup constantly. About 120 people use it each week, mostly students and families planning a grocery run.
