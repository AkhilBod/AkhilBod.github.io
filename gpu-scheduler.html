GPU scheduler

A Kubernetes scheduler that autoscales GPU workloads and releases idle GPUs. Won the Nutanix US and EMEA intern hackathon.

Event
Nutanix intern hackathon, US and EMEA
Result
1st place
Stack
Python, Kubernetes

GPUs are the most expensive thing in most clusters, and a lot of the time they sit there doing nothing. A job finishes, or a notebook gets left open overnight, and the GPU stays reserved anyway.

For the Nutanix intern hackathon, I built a scheduler on Kubernetes that watches GPU workloads, scales them up when there is demand, and releases GPUs that have gone idle so they can be handed to something else or shut off. It's written in Python.

It won first place across the US and EMEA intern cohorts.
